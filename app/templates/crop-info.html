प्रमुख फसलों, उनकी मिट्टी और जलवायु की जानकारी

🌾
Rice (धान)

Soil: Clayey and loamy soil

Temperature: 20–35°C

Rainfall: High rainfall, generally 1000–2000 mm

Season: Kharif

Rice is a major food crop that generally requires plenty of water.

🌽
Maize (मक्का)

Soil: Well-drained loamy soil

Temperature: 18–32°C

Rainfall: Moderate rainfall

Season: Mainly Kharif

Maize is used as food, animal feed and for industrial purposes.

🌿
Jute (जूट)

Soil: Fertile alluvial soil

Temperature: 24–35°C

Rainfall: High rainfall, generally 1500–2500 mm

Season: Kharif

Jute is a natural fibre crop used for making bags, ropes and textiles.

🌻
Cotton (कपास)

Soil: Deep black and well-drained soil

Temperature: 21–30°C

Rainfall: Moderate rainfall

Season: Kharif

Cotton is an important commercial fibre crop.

🍬
Sugarcane (गन्ना)

Soil: Fertile loamy soil

Temperature: 20–35°C

Rainfall: Around 750–1500 mm

Season: Long-duration crop

Sugarcane is a major commercial crop used for producing sugar and jaggery.

🌱
Chickpea (चना)

Soil: Well-drained loamy soil

Temperature: 10–30°C

Rainfall: Low to moderate rainfall

Season: Rabi

Chickpea is a pulse crop and an important source of plant protein.

🌼
Mustard (सरसों)

Soil: Loamy and well-drained soil

Temperature: 10–25°C

Rainfall: Low to moderate rainfall

Season: Rabi

Mustard is an oilseed crop used for edible oil production.

🥔
Kidney Beans (राजमा)

Soil: Well-drained loamy soil

Temperature: 15–25°C

Rainfall: Moderate rainfall

Season: Varies by region

Kidney beans are a protein-rich pulse crop.

🍎
Apple (सेब)

Soil: Well-drained loamy soil

Temperature: Cool temperate climate

Rainfall: Region-dependent

Season: Perennial fruit crop

Apple is a temperate fruit crop that needs suitable winter chilling conditions.
