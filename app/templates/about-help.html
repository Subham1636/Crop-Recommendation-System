About Crop Recommendation System

Crop Recommendation System एक Machine Learning आधारित वेब एप्लिकेशन है जो मिट्टी और मौसम से संबंधित जानकारी के आधार पर किसानों को उपयुक्त फसल का सुझाव देने में मदद करता है।

इसका उद्देश्य किसानों को उपलब्ध soil parameters और environmental conditions के आधार पर बेहतर crop selection में सहायता देना है।

Our Main Features

Machine Learning

Five ML algorithms are available for crop prediction: Random Forest, Decision Tree, Naive Bayes, SVM and KNN.

Soil Analysis

Enter Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium and pH values to get a crop recommendation.

Weather Parameters

Temperature, humidity and rainfall are also considered by the prediction system.

Prediction History

Previous crop predictions can be stored and viewed through the history section.

Crop Information

Learn about different crops, their growing seasons, soil requirements and climate conditions.

User Accounts

Register and log in to access the dashboard and use the crop recommendation features.

How to Use the System

  1. Register a new account or log in.
  2. Open the Crop Recommendation section.
  3. Enter your soil N, P, K and pH values.
  4. Enter temperature, humidity and rainfall values.
  5. Select your preferred Machine Learning algorithm.
  6. Click the prediction button to get the recommended crop.
  7. Visit Prediction History to view saved results.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. What is a crop recommendation system?

It is a system that uses soil and weather parameters to suggest a suitable crop using Machine Learning.

Q2. Which parameters are required?

Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium, temperature, humidity, pH and rainfall.

Q3. Which algorithms are available?

Random Forest, Decision Tree, Naive Bayes, SVM and KNN.

Q4. Can I see my previous predictions?

Yes. Open the Prediction History section to view your saved prediction records.

Q5. Is the prediction guaranteed to be accurate?

No. Predictions are model-based suggestions. Actual crop suitability also depends on local conditions, soil testing, irrigation and farming practices.
